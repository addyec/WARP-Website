Water Acidification Research Project - Gahanna Lincoln UnderWater Robotics Club

WARP stands for water acidification research project, the research done by Gahanna URWC is apart of a larger global movement for praticipating teams of MATE ROV to bring greater awarness to the issue of water acidification (the process or water turning more acidic and often harmful): more is detailed on the website.



Photo from project.



Gahanna Lincoln UWRC collected data for WARP by utilizing pool grade pH sticks and metal thermometers. Around every month, a member would go to Rocky Fork use a pH strip and two thermometers (averaged for data purposes) to record pH and water temperature data. Further, this member would take photograph(s) of the creek and note details about the creek's environment. Additional weather and environmental data being sourced from the Apple Weather App, and once from Weather Underground.

    URWC tests for WARP were conducted at local Gahanna creek: Rocky Fork. One of the main purposes of WARP is to provide data for Gahanna residents, for that reason local testing was conducted to collect data for local residents. This experiment sought to determine for the Gahanna community if water acidification was a local problem, and the conclusion of this experiment has support that water acidification is not a pressing problem.
